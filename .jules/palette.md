## 2026-11-04 - Accessibility Attributes for Interactive and Dynamic Elements
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons must include a 'title' attribute alongside 'aria-label' to assist sighted users, and dynamically updating content containers require 'aria-live' attributes (e.g., 'aria-live="polite"') for screen readers.
**Action:** Always add 'title' to icon-only buttons and 'aria-live="polite"' to dynamically updating elements.
